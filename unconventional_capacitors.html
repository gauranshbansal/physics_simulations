✦ Unconventional Geometries: Beyond Classical Symmetry
Capacitance is fundamentally a Dirichlet boundary-value problem of Laplace's equation . When plates are tilted, wires run parallel, cylinders shift off-center, or surfaces form confocal ellipses and coaxial cones, the geometry reshapes the electric field lines into rich non-uniform topologies.

Coaxial Cone-in-Cone
APEX DIVERGENCE

FRINGE-FIELD DRIVE
