Model notes

Geometry, then projection. Points live in four Euclidean coordinates. Plane rotations preserve their 4D length; perspective maps them onto this screen.

Read each model on its own terms. Hopf fibers lie on the unit 3-sphere. Lorenz is a numerically integrated 3D flow with an added display coordinate. Black-hole motion is a stylized illustration, not a relativistic geodesic solver.

Interaction changes the field. Holding or dragging applies an artistic attraction that can deform the ideal structures. Perturb resamples the particles and briefly applies that effect.
