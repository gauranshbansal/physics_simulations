"I love figuring out how things are built to make us feel something."

I don't box myself into neat titles. I write, design, immerse myself in virtual worlds and cinema, practice yoga, read voraciously, and explore first principles. Life is not about choosing a narrow lane—it is all about Experience.

The Origin: The Father, The Screen & The Genesis of Wonder

Before I ever penned a manuscript or designed a brand, my world was shaped by a father who lived years ahead of his time. In an era when personal computers were a rare novelty in most households, my father set up a PC specifically for us.

He was an avid gamer and cinema enthusiast. Our home was filled with stacks of CDs, DVDs, and a Game Boy he brought into my hands. Together, we explored countless worlds—racing down neon streets in Road Rash, feeling the gritty cinematic slow-motion noir of Max Payne, and venturing through Kanto in Pokémon FireRed.

We watched thousands of movies together across every genre and era, paired with every legendary animated show on television—from the inventive wonders of Doraemon and the unfiltered humor of Shinchan to the expansive alien lore of Ben 10. That childhood was not passive entertainment; it was the bedrock of a boundless imagination, visual literacy, and an innate sense of narrative pacing.

That fire has only grown stronger. I am a gamer through and through—from retro pixel-art gems to conquering the uncompromising mechanical precision, environmental storytelling, and lore of masterworks like Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice and Elden Ring. Gaming taught me how atmosphere, challenge, sound design, and player agency converge to create genuine emotion.

Inner Stillness, Yoga & Voracious Reading

While the external world is filled with noise and output, my anchor lies in the profound stillness of inner exploration. Guided by the practices of the Isha Foundation, classical yoga is the discipline through which I align body, mind, and energy.

To create art, write stories, or analyze complex systems without understanding the very instrument of perception is incomplete. Yoga provides the clarity to experience life without cognitive distortion, to sit in absolute stillness, and to experience consciousness as an immediate, vibrant reality.

Hand in hand with this is an insatiable appetite for books—diving voraciously across philosophy, human psychology, advertising, literature, neuroscience, and science. Reading is the ultimate form of mental teleportation: distilling a lifetime of someone else’s refined consciousness into hours of intense contemplation.

"Understanding is not recognizing things. It is recognizing what survives when the things are gone — finding form that survives its embodiment."

The Synthesis

When all these streams converge, life ceases to be a checklist of tasks—it becomes an art of pure experience.
